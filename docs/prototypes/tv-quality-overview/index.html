Series quality overview

Three ways to answer “is this show consistently good?” using the same three-season dataset. The first stays closest to your reference; the others prioritize season comparison or trend.

Option A

Episode matrix

Closest to the reference. Scan every episode by season and inspect individual cells.

Option B

Season scorecards

Lead with averages, range, and consistency; keep episode detail secondary.
Option C

Quality trajectory

Show momentum and volatility across seasons through a comparative line chart.
